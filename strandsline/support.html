StrandsLine support

StrandsLine is an unofficial letter-grid worksheet for iPhone and iPad. It is not affiliated with The New York Times, and it does not provide answers.

Using the worksheet

  • Draw through letters, then tap the last letter again to lock a mark.
  • The color pills choose the mark. Tap the selected pill again for a pencil ring.
  • Tap the eye to show dictionary words for the letters you have highlighted. Press and hold the eye to look back along the path.
  • Open the calendar to move between days. A purple ring is a saved day. Press and hold a purple day to delete that save.
  • The practice board in the tour is not written to your days.

iCloud

Settings can sync saved boards through your private iCloud. Turn it off to keep boards only on this device. Fully quit and reopen the App after changing that switch.

Tips

The heart opens an optional tip jar. Apple handles the payment. A tip does not add features or answers. You can tip more than once.
