Privacy Policy for StrandsLine

StrandsLine · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes how the iPhone and iPad app StrandsLine (“the App”) handles information. The App is an unofficial worksheet and is not affiliated with The New York Times.

Summary

  • No ads. No third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your marks stay on your device. Optional iCloud sync uses your Apple ID’s private iCloud, not a shared database.
  • The App does not ask you to create an account with the developer.
  • Optional tips are processed by Apple. A tip does not unlock features or answers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store on your device:

  • Saved boards — the letter grid, the clue, your color marks, and the undo timeline for a day you opened. The puzzle’s answer list is not stored with that day.
  • A local word list — words this device learned while opening a board, kept in a file on the device so a later highlight can recognize them. That file is not uploaded.
  • Settings — choices such as appearance, whether the eye is on, and whether saved-board iCloud sync is on. These use the system preferences store on the device.

iCloud

If Sync saved boards with iCloud is on and the device is signed into iCloud, saved boards may sync through Apple’s private CloudKit for your Apple ID. The developer does not have a public copy of your boards and cannot open another person’s private database. You can turn sync off in Settings. Boards already on the device stay there. Apple’s handling of iCloud data follows Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings.

Letter grids loaded over the network

When you open a day that is not already saved on the device, the App requests that day’s letter grid over HTTPS. The response can contain more than the grid. The App keeps the letters and the clue for your worksheet. It does not store that response’s answer list as the day’s answers. Words the game accepted can be added to the local word list described above.

Tips

Tips are optional consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes the payment. The App does not receive your card number. A tip does not add features or answers.

What the App does not do

  • It does not use the camera or the photo library.
  • It does not include advertising or a tracking SDK.
  • The privacy manifest shipped with the App declares no tracking and no collected data types. The only required-reason API it declares is reading its own preferences (UserDefaults), reason CA92.1.

Deleting your marks

Press and hold a saved day in the calendar to delete that day’s marks from the device. If a bonus board is saved, you can delete just that board. Deleting the App removes its local files. Data already in your private iCloud follows your Apple ID settings.
