StrandsLine

An unofficial worksheet for a letter grid. For iPhone and iPad.

Mark the words you find, pencil in a hunch, and look up dictionary words the letters you have highlighted can still spell. StrandsLine keeps your marks. It does not supply the puzzle’s answers.

What it is

  • Color fills and pencil rings on the letters you choose.
  • A word list for the highlight you drew, not a list of the day’s answers.
  • A calendar of days you have opened, with your marks kept on the device.
  • Optional private iCloud sync for those saved boards.
  • An optional tip. A tip does not unlock features or answers.

StrandsLine is not affiliated with The New York Times. It is not the official game, and it is not an answers app.
